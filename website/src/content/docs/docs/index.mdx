cloofy plays Spotify and YouTube Music in the terminal, one of them or both side by side. It plays the music itself, with no official app in the background: Spotify through librespot, as a Spotify Connect device you can pick from your phone, and YouTube Music through mpv, with or without an account.

It looks and works like the desktop client you know: your Home feed, your library in a sidebar, cover art, synced lyrics, and on Spotify the DJ talking between tracks.

![cloofy in a terminal: the Home page with cover art, a playlist with the playing song, and the now-playing view with synced lyrics](/screenshots/hero.webp)

## Try it first

```sh
cloofy demo
```

The demo opens the full interface with a sample library: real albums and artists, and a few made-up playlists. It needs no account and plays nothing, so it's a quick way to see how cloofy looks in your terminal. Every picture on this site comes from it.

## What you need

- **macOS or Linux**, and a terminal with true color. Cover art shows as real images in [most terminals](/docs/terminals/), as blocks in the rest.
- **For Spotify:** Spotify Premium and a free Spotify developer app of your own. The installer sets it up with you in about two minutes.
- **For YouTube Music:** nothing but [mpv](https://mpv.io), [yt-dlp](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) and [deno](https://deno.com), which the installer offers to install. No account needed.
